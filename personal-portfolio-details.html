Project Overview

This e-commerce platform was developed for a growing retail business looking to expand their online presence. The project aimed to create a seamless shopping experience while providing robust backend management capabilities.

The platform handles thousands of products, processes hundreds of orders daily, and integrates with multiple payment gateways and shipping providers.

Challenges & Solutions

Performance Optimization

Challenge: The site needed to handle high traffic during sales events with thousands of concurrent users.

Solution: Implemented server-side rendering, CDN integration, database indexing, and implemented caching strategies that improved load times by 70%.

Security Implementation

Challenge: Ensuring secure payment processing and protecting user data from potential threats.

Solution: Implemented HTTPS, JWT authentication, input validation, rate limiting, and regular security audits. Integrated with PCI-compliant payment processors.
